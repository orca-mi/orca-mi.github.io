ORCA-MI

Open Radiology & Computational Analysis · Molecular Imaging

Agile intelligence, deep insight

A fast imaging workstation for Windows, made for nuclear medicine and molecular imaging, with full CT and MR support: viewing and fusion, 3D, AI segmentation, PET / SPECT quantification, dosimetry, registration and DICOM to NIfTI. Free for research and education.

⬇ Download for Windows Portable (zip)
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · no administrator rights needed · always the latest version (all versions)
⬇ Ubuntu / Linux (test version)
ORCA-MI 0.1.3 for Ubuntu 20.04 or newer, 64-bit: unpack and start ./orca-mi. Same program, updated less often; no session recording, system monitor or automatic updates.
🌐 Open ORCA-MI Web (in your browser)
Nothing to install (Chrome, Edge or Firefox): viewer for DICOM and NIfTI, PET/CT fusion, 3D MIP and the NEMA IEC phantom analysis. Your images stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.
▶ Video tutorials on YouTube
Short videos, one feature each: installing, requesting the licence, loading images and more.
Getting started
  1. Run the installer. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”: More info → Run anyway.
  2. ORCA-MI runs as a 7-day trial. Request your free licence in the program: Help → Licence → Request your free licence.
  3. Load the .lic file you receive by e-mail. Updates are offered by ORCA-MI itself.
